About

A life at the meeting point of faith, education, scholarship, and service.

I am a pastor-teacher, educator, scholar, and researcher in the Philippines. My work brings together Christian ministry, religious studies, ethics, education, human dignity, peace, community life, and questions of public responsibility.

Teaching and ministry have placed me in classrooms, churches, communities, and institutions where ideas must eventually meet real human lives. My academic work therefore asks not only what we should believe, but also what we owe one another and how institutions should act when human welfare is at stake.

I am also developing a long-term program of public leadership formation grounded in study, service, responsible speech, constitutional government, institution-building, and concern for future generations.

Human Dignity Public institutions should treat every person as possessing worth that cannot be reduced to utility, status, or political convenience.
Responsible Service Authority should be understood first as responsibility for persons, institutions, and the common life.
Truthful Scholarship Academic work should be careful with evidence, fair to competing arguments, and clear about what its sources can actually support.
Formation Before Position Public leadership begins long before public office: through disciplined study, character, service, and institutional trust.

Public Leadership Formation

Preparing for responsibility before authority.

01

02

03

Work respectfully across religious, academic, professional, social, and political boundaries.
