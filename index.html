About

Faith, education, scholarship, service, and responsibility.

I am a pastor-teacher, educator, scholar, and researcher in the Philippines. My work brings together Christian ministry, religious studies, ethics, education, human dignity, peace, community life, and public responsibility.

Teaching and ministry have placed me in classrooms, churches, communities, and institutions where ideas must eventually meet real human lives.

My continuing work also includes public leadership formation and creation care: learning how persons, institutions, communities, and governments can act responsibly toward people, society, and the natural world.

Human Dignity Every person possesses worth that should not depend on status, utility, wealth, or political convenience.
Responsible Service Authority should first be understood as responsibility toward people and institutions.
Care for Creation Human flourishing cannot be separated from responsible care for the living world we inhabit.
Formation Before Position Public leadership begins through study, service, character, and earned trust.

Public Leadership Formation

Preparing for responsibility before authority.

Public leadership is approached here as a long-term discipline of study, service, judgment, institution-building, and concern for future generations.

01

Study

Philippine history, constitutional government, law, economics, policy, diplomacy, and public administration.

02

Serve

Build a genuine record of work with communities and institutions.

03

Write

Contribute serious arguments on public questions through scholarship, essays, lectures, and policy work.

04

Build Trust

Work respectfully across religious, academic, professional, social, and political differences.

Public leadership formation is not a declaration of political candidacy. It is a commitment to becoming better prepared for public responsibility wherever service may eventually be required.
