About Lemuel Dayrit Danganan

Lemuel Dayrit Danganan is a pastor-teacher, scholar of religion and philosophy, educator, researcher, and advocate for creation care and responsible public life.

Scholarship and teaching

This personal website brings together work in philosophy of religion, ethics, political thought, theology, and public life. The teaching section lists undergraduate and graduate courses across the institutions identified on the home page.

The scholarship section links to research records. The recent research page distinguishes conference records and preprints from peer-reviewed journal articles.

Ministry and creation care

Pastoral care, spiritual formation, community service, ecological stewardship and wildlife photography are further areas of work represented here. Explore the bird photography gallery and sermons and Bible studies.
