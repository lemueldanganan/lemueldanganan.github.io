Privacy

Updated 19 October 2026. This notice covers the personal website at lemueldanganan.github.io and its mirror at lemueldanganan-github-io.vercel.app.

Hosting and contact

GitHub Pages and Vercel deliver this static website. Requests to these hosts can include your IP address, browser information and requested page. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Vercel’s privacy policy.

There is no contact form on this site. Email links open your email application. Information you choose to send by email is used to read and respond to your inquiry. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. Privacy inquiries can be sent to proflem.dangana@gmail.com.

Google advertising and cookies

The home page and research records page include the Google AdSense script. Its presence does not mean Google has approved this site or that ads are currently served. Third parties, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor’s prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also visit aboutads.info to manage participating third-party vendors’ personalized advertising cookies. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Video, counters and external services

The home page embeds a YouTube video, which sends requests to YouTube when loaded. See Google’s privacy policy. Visitor count images load from hits.sh, with counterapi.com as a backup. These requests can disclose your IP address and browser information to the provider. Counters represent page views; the displayed country flags are decorative, not measured visitor locations.

Links to scholarly repositories, social platforms and other websites take you to services with their own privacy practices. Browser controls can limit cookies; some third-party features may be affected.
